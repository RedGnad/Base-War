# Attributions

Everything in `assets/` (models, sounds, textures, icons, avatar clips) is generated by
the scripts in `tools/`, with two exceptions listed below. The pistol, `assets/Models/gun.glb`, comes from the open Decentraland
model catalog (`models.dclregenesislabs.xyz`).

## Jump and glider glyphs

`tools/ui/vendor/joypad/double-jump-normal.svg` and `glide-normal.svg` are copied verbatim
from the Decentraland mobile explorer, https://github.com/decentraland/godot-explorer
(path `godot/src/ui/components/organisms/joypad/`, main branch, fetched 4 September 2026),
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. They are rasterised by
`tools/ui/build-thumb-icons.py` so the scene's jump disc shows exactly the pictures the client
would have shown. Details in `tools/ui/vendor/joypad/NOTICE.md`.
